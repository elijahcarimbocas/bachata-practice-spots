Before you go

The $3 to $6 day passes get you into a gym or fitness room, not a private room. Ask the front desk if two people can practice in a corner. Some will say yes, some won't.

Outdoors, stick to smooth concrete or pavers. Turns don't work on grass, and rough pavers chew up dance shoes.

Most Gilbert and Chandler parks close at 10 or 10:30 PM. Tempe Beach Park and Kiwanis Park stay open until midnight, and Soleri Bridge Plaza never closes.

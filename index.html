    Day passes

    The $3 to $6 passes cover a gym or fitness room you share. Some front desks will let two people practice in a corner and some won't.

    Surface

    Outdoors, look for smooth concrete or pavers. Turns don't work on grass, and rough pavers chew up dance shoes.

    Closing time

    Most Gilbert and Chandler parks close at 10 or 10:30 PM. Tempe Beach Park and Kiwanis Park go until midnight. Soleri Bridge Plaza never closes.

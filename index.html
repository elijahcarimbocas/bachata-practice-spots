Filter by type and city. Day passes get you a shared gym or fitness floor, so ask the front desk before you set up.

    All of them at once

    Day passes

    The $1 to $6 passes cover a gym or fitness room you share. Some front desks will let two people practice in a corner and some won't.

    Surface

    Outdoors, look for smooth concrete or pavers. Turns don't work on grass, and rough pavers chew up dance shoes.

    Closing time

    Most Gilbert and Chandler parks close at 10 or 10:30 PM. Dr. A.J. Chandler Park is off the list while it's under renovation into 2027. Kiwanis Park goes until midnight. Soleri Bridge Plaza never closes.

    Rec center rates

    Every city rec center we checked, with the day pass and the cheapest room. We pay non-resident rates everywhere except Scottsdale for Mark and Mesa for Maddie. Observed means we read it on the city's own page or fee PDF.
